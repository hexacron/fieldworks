How to read this
  • Origins are where data physically arises; methods are how providers acquire it; recipes show which methods build each kind of provider; the pipeline turns raw collection into products.
  • Every method opens a playbook: operating steps, tools, economics, pitfalls, source defenses, law and named vendors.
  • Ratings are editorial, 1–3: cost, scale, freshness, defensibility, legal exposure.
  • Vendor evidence: public documented by the vendor, reported press/courts/regulators, inferred industry-typical, unconfirmed.
Scope & caveats

Compiled in 2026 from general domain knowledge; vendor practices, prices and case law change quickly. Inclusion is not endorsement, and nothing here is legal advice.

Underground entries document monitoring, verification and processing, and how covert-collection and purchasing decisions are governed. They do not cover buying from criminal sellers, obtaining logs from criminal channels, or passing vetting in criminal communities.
